Partner questions

Which laboratories do you work with?+

We work with NATA-accredited laboratories, including 4Cyte and Australian Clinical Labs, and route each order to suit your patients and panels.

Can patients see our brand rather than yours?+

Yes. With white-label pathology, patients order your panels under your brand, and we operate in the background.

What if a patient is outside your home visit area?+

They can attend any of more than 2,000 partner collection centres using the same order. We provide a coverage report for your patient postcodes before you go live.
