Questions

Do I need a referral?+

No. We issue the pathology request form with your order.

How should I prepare?+

Drink water as normal and wear a top with sleeves that roll up easily. Some tests require fasting. If you are unsure, read our preparation guide or contact us before your collection.

Is this covered by Medicare?+

This is a private test ordered without a GP referral, so Medicare rebates do not apply.

Can I share my results with my GP?+

Yes. Your report is yours to forward to any doctor.
