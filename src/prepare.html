Preparing for your home visit.

A few simple steps help your nurse complete the collection quickly and comfortably.

The day before
  • Check your reminder message for fasting instructions.
  • Keep your doctor's request form somewhere easy to find.
  • Ask your doctor whether to take your usual medication before the test.
On the morning
  • Drink water, even when fasting. It makes collection easier.
  • Wear a top with sleeves that roll up easily.
  • Have photo ID and your Medicare card ready.
When your nurse arrives
  • You will receive an SMS when they are on the way.
  • A table and chair in a well-lit spot is all they need.

Do I need to fast?

Only some tests require fasting. Your request form, booking confirmation or test page will tell you.

Best time to bookEarly-morning slots suit fasting tests.

What if my nurse is running late?+

We will let you know by SMS straight away with an updated arrival time.

I feel faint during blood tests. What should I do?+

Tell your nurse when they arrive. They can collect while you lie down, and will take their time.

When will I get my results?+

Referred tests go to your requesting doctor. Tests ordered with us are emailed as a doctor-reviewed report.
