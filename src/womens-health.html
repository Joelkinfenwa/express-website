Hormone panel Nurse confirmed · Wednesday 8:00 am

Hormone care involves regular, carefully timed tests.

Women's health services often need bloods at specific times and repeated over months. Fitting those around work and family is where patients fall behind.

Express brings a registered nurse to the patient at an exact time, or the patient walks in to a partner centre. Results go to your clinicians, and your team can see every order from booking to result.

The patient journey

From order to result, inside your service.

  1. 01Doctor ordersYour clinician selects the tests or panel required.
  2. 02Patient booksThe patient, or your team on their behalf, books a home visit time or a nearby centre.
  3. 03Nurse collectsA registered nurse arrives at the confirmed time and delivers the samples to a NATA-accredited laboratory.
  4. 04Results returnResults are sent to the requesting doctor and can be returned to your system.

Common questions

Can we use our own panels?+

Yes. With white-label pathology we set up your panels under your brand, and patients order them through your service.

Can patients request a female nurse?+

Patients can note a preference for a female nurse when booking, and we honour it wherever possible.

Can results flow back into our platform?+

Yes. Results can be returned through the API to your system or EMR, as well as to the requesting doctor.
