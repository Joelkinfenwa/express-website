How this runs behind the scenes

After checkout, patients book a real appointment time through Doorstep, Express Pathology's booking engine — the same infrastructure running home collections for telehealth and longevity partners across Australia.

Exact-time scheduling

Patients choose a real appointment time from live nurse availability in their postcode — a network covering 1,100+ postcodes nationally.

Automated confirmation

The booking is matched to a qualified collector, confirmed instantly, and the patient receives an automated reminder 24 hours before the visit.

Collection to results

Request forms travel with the booking, samples are lodged with an accredited laboratory the same day, and results are returned to Baseline+.
