    Ordering questions

    Do I need a referral from my GP?+

    No. When you order, we issue the pathology request form for you.

    Is this covered by Medicare?+

    These are private tests ordered without a GP referral, so Medicare rebates do not apply. If your doctor has already given you a request form, you can book a nurse to collect it.

    Do any tests need fasting?+

    Some do. If you are unsure, read our preparation guide or contact us before your collection.

    Can I share my results with my GP?+

    Yes. Your report is yours to forward to any doctor.

    I cannot find the test I need.+

    Try building your own test, or contact us and we will check whether we can add it.
