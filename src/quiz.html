Common questions

Do I need a referral or a GP visit?

No. Your pathology request form is issued with your order, so you can go straight to collection.

Is this covered by Medicare?

No. Private tests ordered without a referral are not covered by Medicare. The price you see is the full price, with doctor review included.

What if a nurse cannot come to my area?

Enter your postcode at checkout. If we cannot arrange a home visit, you can use any of our 2,000+ partner collection centres or receive a full refund.

Do I need to fast?

Some panels need an 8 to 12 hour fast, with water only. Your confirmation email explains what applies to your test, and our preparation guide covers the details.

What do I receive, and can I share it with my GP?

An Australian doctor reviews your results, and your report is emailed to you with reference ranges. The report is yours, and you can forward it to any doctor.
