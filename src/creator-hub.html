The creative brief

Your voice, your style — that is why we partnered with you. The structure below is what performs; make it yours.

Video 1 — your test, start to finish

The one every creator films first. Booking it on your phone → the collection (centre or nurse, both film well — the blood draw takes seconds) → your report arriving → one honest takeaway. Share only what you are comfortable sharing; "my iron was lower than I expected" works without a single number.

Hooks that work
  • "I thought I was just tired all the time. So I stopped guessing."
  • "You track your sleep, your steps, your training — but not your blood?"
  • "Training for my first Hyrox, so I got baseline bloods first."
  • "No GP referral, no waiting room. This took ten minutes."
  • "This report told me more than my last three check-ups."
  • "The old way: book a GP, get a referral, wait. What I did instead:"
Format

Vertical 9:16 Reels, 20–45 seconds, shot on your phone — natural light, real locations, no studio polish. Say your code out loud and put your link in your bio. Post to Instagram (and TikTok if you are on it).

The rules — health content is regulated
  • Every post discloses the partnership — use the paid-partnership label and #ad.
  • Your genuine experience only. Never claim a test diagnoses, prevents or treats any condition, and never give medical advice.
  • A blood test is information, not a substitute for seeing a doctor — do not present it as one.
  • Show your own results only, and only as much as you choose to.
  • Send us the draft before it goes live. We check the health-advertising rules only — we will not touch your creative.
