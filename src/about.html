About Express

We bring pathology to people, and to the services that care for them.

Why we started

Today, Express operates a national network of mobile collectors and partner collection centres, working with NATA-accredited laboratories. Patients book us directly, and clinics, telehealth services and health platforms use us as the pathology layer behind their own care.

What we hold ourselves to

On time, every time

An exact appointment is a promise. We plan our network around keeping it.

Clinical standards first

Trained, verified collectors and accredited laboratories, without exception.

Care in every visit

A calm, unhurried experience in the place the patient is most comfortable.

Built to plug in

Our technology is designed so partners can offer pathology without building it.
